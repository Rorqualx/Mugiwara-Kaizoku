# How existing manga/comic library managers model editions and map volumes to chapters

Research date: 2026-10-11. Source code was read from the default branches on that date (Kavita `develop`, Komf `master`, Komga `master`, Stump `main`, Suwayomi-Server `master`, Mihon `main`, Mylar3 `master`). Where a claim comes from code, the link is to the file; where it comes from an issue, the issue state and date are given.

## 1. How do Komga, Kavita, Mylar3, Suwayomi, Mihon (and trackers), LANraragi, Calibre, Ubooquity and Stump model series / volume / chapter / special / edition, and how do they parse volume vs chapter numbers?

### Takeaway
Only Kavita has a real Series → Volume → Chapter hierarchy with sentinel volumes for loose chapters and specials, and only Kavita has (as of July 2026) a persisted edition pointer on the series; every other tool is flat (series → book, or manga → chapter) and treats a different edition as a different series. Nobody models "chapter N belongs to volume V of edition E" as data: a chapter's volume is whatever the filename says.

### Cited Findings

**Kavita (the only three-level model)**
- Entities are `Series` → `Volume` → `Chapter`; `Chapter` has `VolumeId`, and `Volume` has `SeriesId` and a list of chapters. — [Volume.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Volume.cs), [Chapter.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Chapter.cs)
- A volume stores `Name` ("A String representation of the volume number. Allows for floats. Can also include a range (1-2)"), plus `MinNumber` and `MaxNumber` floats; the old int `Number` is marked obsolete ("Removed in v0.7.13.8 ... we need the ability to have 0.5 volumes render on the UI"). — [Volume.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Volume.cs)
- A chapter stores `Range`, `Number`, `MinNumber`, `MaxNumber`, `SortOrder` (+ `SortOrderLocked`), `IsSpecial`, `Title`, `Count`, `TotalCount`, `AlternateNumber`, `AlternateCount`, `StoryArcNumber`, `ISBN`. — [Chapter.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Chapter.cs)
- Sentinel values carry the "no volume" and "special" cases: `LooseLeafVolume = "-100000"`, `DefaultChapter = "-100000"`, `SpecialVolume = "100000"`. A loose chapter file lives in the loose-leaf volume; a whole-volume file is a volume holding one default chapter; specials live in the special volume. — [ParserConstants.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Constants/ParserConstants.cs)
- Docs: "In order for something to be parsed as having a volume, a volume must be on the filename" and "a chapter marker is required for a file to be parsed as anything other than a loose leaf." Recognised volume markers include `v1`, `vol 1`, `volume 01`, `Vol 7.5`, `t2`/`tome 2`, `卷2`, `2巻`, `Том 2`; `S01` is a volume only for manga. Chapter markers include `c1`, `ch.1`, `chapter 1`, `話`, `话`, `화`; a bare number is a chapter (`Beelzebub_53[KSH].zip` → 53); trailing `b` means .5. Ranges (`Vol. 1-5`, `c001-006`) are supported. If two volume markers are present Kavita uses the first. — [Kavita wiki: Manage Files](https://wiki.kavitareader.com/guides/scanner/managefiles/)
- Series membership is decided from filenames, not folders, but "Kavita requires that each series be in its folder and that no files are at root level of the library." Anything in `()` is stripped from the series name; `{ }` is the documented way to keep two same-named series apart. — [Kavita wiki: Manage Files](https://wiki.kavitareader.com/guides/scanner/managefiles/)
- Specials: a file is a special when a series name parses but no volume or chapter does, or when the name contains `SP01` etc. Words such as Annual, TPB, Omnibus, One Shot, Compendium do not make a special on their own. — [Kavita wiki: Manage Files](https://wiki.kavitareader.com/guides/scanner/managefiles/)
- The parser's edition regex is narrow: `\b(?:Omnibus(?:\s?Edition)?|Uncensored)\b`, with comments citing `Tenjo Tenge {Full Contact Edition} v01` and `To Love Ru v01 Uncensored`. `ParseEdition` only returns the matched word. — [Parser.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Services/Scanner/Parser.cs)
- Editions as relationships: the `RelationKind` enum includes `Edition = 13` alongside Prequel, Sequel, SpinOff, Adaptation, SideStory, Character, Contains, Other, AlternativeSetting, AlternativeVersion, Doujinshi, Parent, `Annual = 14` and a MangaBaka-only `Cameo = 16`. The wiki defines Edition as "When another series is a special edition or colored edition". So a coloured or omnibus edition is a second Series linked to the first. — [RelationKind.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Enums/RelationKind.cs), [Kavita wiki: Relationships](https://wiki.kavitareader.com/guides/features/relationships/)
- Recent (July 2026): PR #4798 "Editions, Volume Metadata, and Ability to Rename Series in the UI" added: "Kavita can now match to editions (Collections on MB) which are alternative versions and come with their own metadata (volume/chapter metadata) and covers." The migration `AddEditionsVolumeCoverAndSeriesNameLock` adds `Series.MangaBakaEditionId`, `Series.NameLocked`, `Series.NormalizedOriginalName` and `Volume.KPlusOverrides`. — [Kavita PR #4798](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/pull/4798), [migration](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Database/Migrations/20260721200907_AddEditionsVolumeCoverAndSeriesNameLock.cs), [Series.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Series.cs)
- The external edition record Kavita consumes has `Id`, `Title`, `Format`, `Language`, `Publisher`, a `Type` of `Chapter | Volume | Other` ("Which type of entries are counted"), `MainCount` ("Number of entries in the main storyline") and `TotalCount` ("Includes extras"). — [ExternalEditionDto.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/DTOs/KavitaPlus/Metadata/ExternalEditionDto.cs)
- `Series` has `Name`, `LocalizedName`, `OriginalName` (each with a normalised form and a lock flag); matching by name checks both name and localized name (`MatchesSeriesByName(nameNormalized, localizedNameNormalized)`). — [Series.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Series.cs)

**Komga (flat: series = folder, book = file)**
- "Komga creates a series for each subfolder of any library" and one book per file; the library "does not represent exactly your folder structure." — [Komga docs: scan, analysis, refresh](https://komga.org/docs/guides/scan-analysis-refresh/)
- `Book` has `name`, `url`, `number: Int`, `seriesId`, `oneshot: Boolean`; `BookMetadata` has a string `number` and a float `numberSort` with per-field locks. There is no volume or chapter field. — [Book.kt](https://github.com/gotson/komga/blob/master/komga/src/main/kotlin/org/gotson/komga/domain/model/Book.kt), [BookMetadata.kt](https://github.com/gotson/komga/blob/master/komga/src/main/kotlin/org/gotson/komga/domain/model/BookMetadata.kt)
- `SeriesMetadata` has `totalBookCount`, `alternateTitles`, `status`, `language`, `publisher`, each with a lock flag; no edition field. — [SeriesMetadata.kt](https://github.com/gotson/komga/blob/master/komga/src/main/kotlin/org/gotson/komga/domain/model/SeriesMetadata.kt)
- ComicInfo import: `Series` + `Volume` become the series title "<Series> (<Volume>)" (the most frequent value across books wins); `Count` max becomes total book count; `SeriesGroup` creates collections; `AlternateSeries` or `StoryArc` create read lists positioned by `AlternateNumber` / `StoryArcNumber`. — [Komga docs: scan, analysis, refresh](https://komga.org/docs/guides/scan-analysis-refresh/)
- "One-Shots are series containing a single book", detected by a configured directory name; series and book expose a boolean `oneshot`. — [Komga docs: One-Shots](https://komga.org/docs/guides/oneshots/)

**Stump (flat, Komga-like, with Mylar-shaped series metadata)**
- `media_metadata` has `number: Decimal`, `volume: i32`, `series`, `series_group`, `story_arc`, `story_arc_number`, `format`, plus `metadata_source`, `metadata_external_id` and `locked_fields`. `series_metadata` has `booktype`, `collects`, `comicid`, `volume`, `total_issues`, `publication_run`. No chapter or edition entity. — [media_metadata.rs](https://github.com/stumpapp/stump/blob/main/crates/models/src/entity/media_metadata.rs), [series_metadata.rs](https://github.com/stumpapp/stump/blob/main/crates/models/src/entity/series_metadata.rs)
- "Nested series" (a library pattern generating sub-series from folder depth) is still an open feature request from February 2024. — [Stump #280](https://github.com/stumpapp/stump/issues/280)

**Mylar3 (ComicVine volume = series; booktype instead of edition)**
- Mylar's `series.json` carries `comicid` ("ComicVine comicid"), `volume` ("volume of the series in relation to other titles (this can be either numerical or the series year)"), `booktype` ("Print, OneShot, TPB or GN") and `collects` ("TPB or GN may collect various single issues/series. This will list them here", each with series, CV comicid and issueid). — [Mylar3 wiki: series.json schema 1.0.1](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/wiki/series.json-schema-%28version-1.0.1%29)
- Filename parser: a token starting `v`, `vol`, `volume` or `part` followed by digits is the volume; `tpb`/`digital tpb` → `TPB`, `gn`/`graphic novel` → `GN`, `hc`/`hardcover` → `HC`; with a volume but no issue number, booktype becomes `'TPB/GN/HC/One-Shot'`; with neither, volume defaults to `v1`. There is no "omnibus" keyword. — [filechecker.py](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/blob/master/mylar/filechecker.py)

**Mihon / Tachiyomi and Suwayomi (flat: manga → chapters from a source)**
- Suwayomi's `ChapterTable` has `name`, `chapter_number: float default -1`, `scanlator`, `sourceOrder`, `manga`; there is no volume column. — [ChapterTable.kt](https://github.com/Suwayomi/Suwayomi-Server/blob/master/server/src/main/kotlin/suwayomi/tachidesk/manga/model/table/ChapterTable.kt)
- Mihon's chapter recogniser strips volume and season tags before reading the chapter number: `volume = \b(?:(?:vol(?:ume)?|season)[^a-z0-9]? *|[vs]\.?)([0-9]+(?:\.[0-9]+)?)\b`; then prefers the number after `ch.`, else the first number. Suffixes map to fractions: "extra" +0.99, "omake" +0.98, "special" +0.97; a single letter a–i adds .1–.9; "part N" becomes a letter. Returns -1.0 when nothing parses. — [ChapterRecognition.kt](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/blob/main/domain/src/main/java/tachiyomi/domain/chapter/service/ChapterRecognition.kt)
- Tracker binding is one row per (manga, tracker): `remoteId`, `lastChapterRead: Double`, `totalChapters`; no volume progress field. Suwayomi's `TrackRecordTable` has the same shape. — [Track.kt](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/blob/main/domain/src/main/java/tachiyomi/domain/track/model/Track.kt), [TrackRecordTable.kt](https://github.com/Suwayomi/Suwayomi-Server/blob/master/server/src/main/kotlin/suwayomi/tachidesk/manga/model/table/TrackRecordTable.kt)
- "Volume support across the app" is open since January 2024: "a 'chapter' in the app is a volume with multiple chapters. This is an issue when using trackers as they only track chapters." A follow-up asks to bind several tracker entries to one manga per volume. — [Mihon #15](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/issues/15), [Mihon #3276](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/issues/3276)

**ComicInfo.xml (the shared interchange format)**
- `Volume`: "Volume is a notion that is specific to US Comics, where the same series can have multiple volumes", referenced by number or year. `Number`: "Number of the book in the series." `Count`: "The total number of books in the series", which "could be different on each book". `Format`: "The original publication's binding format" (TBP, HC, Web, Digital). `SeriesGroup`: "A group or collection the series belongs to." `AlternateSeries/Number/Count` are for crossover arcs. The page gives no guidance for manga volume-versus-chapter. — [Anansi Project: ComicInfo documentation](https://anansi-project.github.io/docs/comicinfo/documentation)

**LANraragi**
- Archives carry namespaced tags and a summary; a `source:` tag lets plugins fetch metadata directly "instead of guessing what the archive is." — [LANraragi docs: Metadata](https://sugoi.gitbook.io/lanraragi/basic-operations/metadata)
- A "Tankoubon" grouping of archives exists and is actively maintained (fixes in 2026); "Comic/Manga Support" as a series model is an open request since October 2021. — [LANraragi #1729](https://github.com/Difegue/LANraragi/issues/1729), [LANraragi #526](https://github.com/Difegue/LANraragi/issues/526)

### Inferences
- The proven design for a mixed library (whole-volume archives plus loose chapters in one series) is Kavita's: one hierarchy, two sentinel volumes, and float min/max ranges on both volume and chapter. Kavita does not reconcile the two: a `v03.cbz` and a `c021.cbz` that is inside volume 3 sit side by side as unrelated rows, because nothing records which chapters a volume contains.
- Every tool resolves "same title, different volume layout" by making a second series. Kavita then links them (`RelationKind.Edition`), and since mid-2026 can point the series at a provider-side edition record whose counts are typed as volumes or chapters. That typed count (`Type`, `MainCount`, `TotalCount`) is the closest existing analogue to a per-edition manifest.
- ComicInfo has no slot for "edition" or "chapters contained"; tools that depend on it (Komga, Stump) inherit that gap. `Format` and `SeriesGroup` are the only fields that can carry it.
- Field-level lock flags (Komga, Kavita, Stump `locked_fields`) are the universal mechanism for keeping a user's correction from being overwritten by the next metadata refresh.

### Gaps
- Calibre (and its comics plugins) and Ubooquity were not researched; no source was fetched for either, so nothing is claimed about them.
- LANraragi's Tankoubon data model (fields, when introduced) was not confirmed from documentation; only its existence is evidenced by issue titles.
- Kavita's parser regex list for volumes and chapters was not quoted verbatim; the markers above are from the wiki, not the code.
- How Kavita detects two different series inside one folder at scan time was not found beyond the one-series-per-folder rule and the PR #4798 line "Massively improved the detect and report a series collision logic and try to give help to user on how to fix." No tool was found that detects a mixed folder by content rather than by parsed name.

## 2. How do metadata agents choose among multiple ComicVine volumes / AniList entries, and how can a user pin or reject a match?

### Takeaway
Matching is title-similarity first everywhere, with provider priority order as the tie-break; none of the agents compare volume or chapter counts to pick between candidates. Pinning is done with an explicit provider ID (a UI "identify", an ID in the folder name, or a CLI flag); durable rejection exists only in Kavita (`DontMatch`, `IsBlacklisted`).

### Cited Findings

**Komf (Komga and Kavita)**
- Metadata comes from the "first positive match in configured providers by order of priority"; default priorities run mangaUpdates 10, mal 20, nautiljon 30, aniList 40, yenPress 50, kodansha 60, viz 70, bookWalker 80, mangaDex 90, bangumi 100, comicVine 110, hentag 120, webtoons 130, mangaBaka 140. With `aggregate` on, later providers only fill fields the earlier one left empty. — [Komf README](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/blob/master/README.md)
- Name matching is Levenshtein with a length-scaled threshold: names of 1–3 characters must match exactly; 4–6 allow distance 1; 7–9 allow 2; longer allow 3. Modes are `EXACT` and `CLOSEST_MATCH`. — [NameSimilarityMatcher.kt](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/blob/master/komf-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/snd/komf/util/NameSimilarityMatcher.kt)
- The match query is `seriesName`, `startYear`, an optional `bookQualifier` (name, number range, cover image) and `seriesFolder`. — [MatchQuery.kt](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/blob/master/komf-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/snd/komf/model/MatchQuery.kt)
- Book numbers come from the filename: volume regexes accept `v`, `t`, `vol. `, `volume ` with optional `-end` range, `第N巻`; chapter regexes accept `c`, `ch. `, `chapter `, `ep. `, `第N話`; text in `[...]` is captured as "extra data". — [BookNameParser.kt](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/blob/master/komf-core/src/commonMain/kotlin/snd/komf/util/BookNameParser.kt)
- Pinning: `POST /{media-server}/identify` takes `libraryId`, `seriesId`, `provider`, `providerSeriesId`; reset endpoints clear metadata per series or library. ComicVine can be pinned by `[cv-{id}]` in the folder name (`comicVineIdFormat`). comicVine is flagged experimental and "can mismatch issue numbers." `libraryType` (MANGA, NOVEL, COMIC, WEBTOON) is a hint "to help better match book numbers." — [Komf README](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/blob/master/README.md)
- The identify path accepts an `edition` string that is passed into book-metadata lookup. — [MetadataService.kt](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/blob/master/komf-mediaserver/src/commonMain/kotlin/snd/komf/mediaserver/metadata/MetadataService.kt)
- Maintainer on choosing among a provider's editions (Nautiljon lists four for Astro Boy): "Currently it's not possible. There's no information to infer the right edition. One of the solutions would be to include the edition in filename", e.g. `Astro Boy - (v01) [Edition Kana]`; later "more or less implemented" in 0.6.1. A user warns "Square Brackets typically contain scanlation groups". The issue is still open. — [Komf #8](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/issues/8)

**Kavita+ (hosted matching service)**
- Series carry `DontMatch` and `IsBlacklisted` booleans, plus name lock flags. — [Series.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Series.cs)
- July 2026 changes: "Always write external ids ... Since we tightened up the auto-matching, the chances of false positives are much less likely"; "Match flow will use external metadata ids if there is no query text"; "Tweaked all logic in the app when finding series to utilize external ids"; volume and chapter covers and metadata now written during the Match flow from MangaBaka/Hardcover. — [Kavita PR #4798](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/pull/4798)
- Volumes carry their own external IDs (`AniListId`, `MalId`, `HardcoverId`, `MetronId`, `ComicVineId`, `MangaBakaId`, `CbrId`) and a `KPlusOverrides` list recording which fields the service set. — [Volume.cs](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/blob/develop/Kavita.Models/Entities/Volume.cs)
- Omnibus and scrobbling: v0.7.14 notes say "Omnibus (Volume 1-2) is now much better supported and Kavita+ will get Volume 2 when you read an Omnibus vs Volume 1 previously." (Quoted from a release-notes mirror, not the GitHub release page.) — [newreleases.io mirror of Kavita v0.7.14](https://newreleases.io/project/github/linuxserver/docker-kavita/release/v0.7.14-ls28)

**Mylar3 (ComicVine)**
- Identity is the ComicVine ComicID; a "mismatched" status exists because the upstream pull-list source "has incorrectly linked a ComicVine ComicID to that mismatched title", which "usually happens when a new volume of a series starts, or the CV name is different". "Before the mismatch tag, Mylar would match it incorrectly, which would result in issues being redownloaded or downloaded that were for the wrong series". Fixes are applied centrally on Mylar's backend; users report them in a Discord channel. — [Mylar3 wiki: The mismatched status](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/wiki/The-mismatched-status)
- All metadata is ComicVine's; nothing can be processed until CV has the issue. — [Mylar3 wiki: Where Mylar gets the metadata from](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/wiki/Where-Mylar-gets-the-metadata-from)
- An open February 2026 request asks for a "Volume Entry Screen without CV/Backend data": user-entered series and issues with a Mylar-specific ID, excluded from CV refresh. — [Mylar3 #1776](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/issues/1776)

**ComicTagger**
- Uses series name, issue number and optionally year to get candidates, then compares "the cover image of an archive with images found online." Named obstacles: alternate covers, duplicate covers, badly named files. — [ComicTagger wiki: UserGuide](https://github.com/comictagger/comictagger/wiki/UserGuide)
- Ambiguity handling: `-i/--interactive` "Interactively query the user when there are multiple matches"; `--noabort` "Don't abort save operation when online match is of low confidence"; `--id ISSUE_ID` "Use the issue ID when searching online", which "Overrides all other metadata." Settings include `id_publisher_filter`, `id_publisher_blacklist`, `always_use_publisher_filter`, `exact_series_matches_first`, `dont_use_year_when_identifying`, `save_on_low_confidence`. — [ComicTagger wiki: UserGuide](https://github.com/comictagger/comictagger/wiki/UserGuide)
- Expected filename shape: `SERIES_NAME vVOLNUM ISSUENUM (of COUNT) (PUBYEAR).ext`; use a volume number or a series year, not both. — [ComicTagger wiki: UserGuide](https://github.com/comictagger/comictagger/wiki/UserGuide)

**Manga-Tagger**
- "Scrapes metadata from Anilist and MyAnimeList (using Jikan)", CBZ only, last advertised build v1.1.5-alpha. — [Manga-Tagger README](https://github.com/Inpacchi/Manga-Tagger/blob/main/README.md)

### Inferences
- The durable-binding pattern is consistent: an external ID stored on the series, used in preference to any title search afterwards (Kavita's "utilize external ids", Komf identify, `[cv-{id}]`, ComicTagger `--id`). Title search is only the bootstrap.
- A stored negative ("this candidate is wrong") is rare. Kavita's flags are per-series switches, not per-candidate rejections; no tool was found that records "provider X id Y is not this series" and consults it on the next match.
- Only ComicTagger uses content (the cover image) to discriminate between candidates. Komf's `BookQualifier` includes a cover image, which suggests cover comparison is at least available to providers, but this was not traced in code.
- No agent uses volume count or chapter count as a signal for choosing between a tankobon entry and an omnibus entry; Komf's maintainer states there is "no information to infer the right edition" without a filename hint.

### Gaps
- The Kavita+ matching wiki page returned 404; the user-facing Match dialog, what "Don't Match" does in practice, and how blacklisting is triggered are not documented here beyond the entity flags.
- Kavita+ matching logic is server-side and closed; its candidate scoring could not be inspected.
- How Komf uses `getExtraData` bracket text and the `edition` parameter to select book metadata was not traced end to end.
- Mylar's own search-result ranking when adding a series (how it orders multiple ComicVine volumes of the same name) was not found in the wiki pages read.
- Manga-Tagger's matching rules, thresholds and maintenance status are not stated in its README.

## 3. How do the TV equivalents solve the same shape of problem, and what is transferable?

### Takeaway
TV tooling splits into two camps: Sonarr picks one canonical numbering (TVDB aired order) and maps everything else onto it with community-maintained translation tables, refusing alternate orders for over a decade; Plex makes an edition a separate library item with its own watch state. Both avoid storing several orderings of the same content side by side.

### Cited Findings
- Sonarr series types: Standard ("Episodes released with SxxEyy pattern"), Daily, and Anime ("Episodes released using an absolute episode number"); "the Anime type does not have any concept of Season Packs or Seasons". — [Servarr wiki: Sonarr FAQ](https://github.com/Servarr/Wiki/blob/master/sonarr/faq.md)
- TheXEM is "a community driven site that lets users create mappings" and "is used to correct differences in episode numbering" and season numbers between release groups and TVDB. Non-anime aliases use a separate scene mapping table: "Requests to this table are added manually by devs, and can take up to 2 weeks to take effect." — [Servarr wiki: Sonarr FAQ](https://github.com/Servarr/Wiki/blob/master/sonarr/faq.md)
- Documented per-show breakages where the one-ordering model fails: American Dad ("typically off by 1 season between releases and TVDb"), Kamen Rider (must use the anthology entry; individual season entries need manual import), Kaleidoscope ("you will need to grab and import the episodes manually"), Money Heist (TVDB keeps the original Spanish airing order while Netflix re-cut it). — [Servarr wiki: Sonarr FAQ](https://github.com/Servarr/Wiki/blob/master/sonarr/faq.md)
- The request "Get DVD ordering from thetvdb" was opened in November 2014, drew 63 comments and is closed; duplicates were filed and closed in 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024 and March 2025 ("Get 'Alternate Order' from thetvdb"). — [Sonarr #255](https://github.com/Sonarr/Sonarr/issues/255), [Sonarr #7732](https://github.com/Sonarr/Sonarr/issues/7732), [Sonarr #6935](https://github.com/Sonarr/Sonarr/issues/6935)
- A Sonarr forum report shows what happens without a per-series order choice: for Money Heist, episodes from TVDB's official and alternate orders were mixed within a season. — [Sonarr forum thread](https://forums.sonarr.tv/t/seasons-episodes-from-official-and-alternate-order-of-thetvdb-are-mixed/23872)
- TheTVDB changes its canonical grouping by policy: split-cour anime, previously "treated each 'cour' as its own season", "will now be treated as a single season", applied retroactively to series such as Fate/Zero and Durarara, with exceptions (Chaika, Silver Spoon) where official numbering treats the second half separately. — [TheTVDB support: split-cour FAQ](https://support.thetvdb.com/kb/faq.php?id=61)
- Kodi's TVDB add-on exposes aired (default), DVD and absolute orders per show; DVD order "will fall back to using Aired Order" when missing, absolute order has no fallback. — [Kodi wiki: Add-on:The TVDB](https://kodi.wiki/view/Add-on:The_TVDB)
- Plex editions (movies only): tagged `{edition-Director's Cut}` in the filename; each edition is its own library item whose "watched status, user ratings, etc. are all tracked separately"; versions (encodes) are merged inside an edition. There is "not support for Editions of TV Shows or Episodes at this time." Requires Plex Pass. — [Plex support: Multiple Editions](https://support.plex.tv/articles/multiple-editions/)
- Jellyfin groups movie versions by filename suffix after the exact folder-name prefix (`Movie (2021) - Directors Cut.mp4`); labels are free text, shown as a version picker on one item. — [Jellyfin docs: Movies](https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/server/media/movies/)

### Inferences
- Transferable from Sonarr/TheXEM: keep one canonical numbering per work (for manga, the chapter number is the natural absolute order, as anime absolute numbering is), and express every other layout (tankobon volume N, omnibus volume M, release-group numbering) as a mapping onto it, not as a peer hierarchy. TheXEM is exactly a table of "numbering scheme A position ↔ scheme B position" keyed by show.
- The cost of that design is visible in the FAQ: mappings are hand-maintained, lag by days to weeks, and each structural oddity becomes a named exception. A self-hosted tool cannot rely on a community table existing for manga editions.
- Transferable from Plex/Jellyfin: two distinct concepts. A "version" is the same content in a different file (merge, one progress state). An "edition" is different content under the same title (separate item or explicit variant). Kavita's Series-plus-`Edition`-relation follows the Plex side. A coloured edition with identical chapter boundaries is closer to a version; a 3-in-1 omnibus changes the volume layout but not the chapters.
- TheTVDB re-grouping split-cour seasons retroactively shows that the upstream "canonical" grouping can change under a tool; stored bindings need to survive a provider renumbering.

### Gaps
- TheXEM's documentation page returned 403, so its data model (origins, per-season names, API endpoints) is described only through Sonarr's FAQ.
- TheTVDB's official definition of season types (aired/DVD/absolute/alternate/regional) and the rules for creating an alternate order were not retrieved; the Kodi page is a secondary description.
- No maintainer statement explaining why Sonarr declines alternate orders was read; the issue bodies and comments of #255 were not fetched, only their existence, dates and state.

## 4. What do issue trackers and docs say are the recurring failure cases?

### Takeaway
The best-documented failures are (1) two editions of one title collapsing into one series, (2) a series holding both omnibus and standard volumes getting metadata for only one layout, (3) numbers inside titles being read as chapters, and (4) trackers that count chapters when the library holds volumes. Maintainers mostly answer with naming conventions, not with model changes.

### Cited Findings
- Edition collapse (Kavita, 2022, closed): with `Magic Knight Rayearth {Omnibus Edition}` and `Magic Knight Rayearth` both present, the omnibus series lost its edition suffix, took "the wrong covers, from the non-omnibus edition", was deleted on cover refresh, and on rescan the omnibus files "replaced v1 and v2" of the standard series and removed volume 3. The same reporter saw it with Neon Genesis Evangelion and `The Ghost in the Shell {HumbleBundle}`. — [Kavita #1513](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/issues/1513)
- Near-identical titles: Kavita changed parenthesis cleaning in July 2026 so that "'Rent-a-(Really-Shy)-Girlfriend' is separate from 'Rent-a-Girlfriend'", i.e. a spin-off had been merging into its parent. — [Kavita PR #4798](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/pull/4798)
- Mixed omnibus and standard in one series (Komf, open, March 2026): "the matching will usually match one or the other (normally the standard release) and ignore populating the book metadata for the other ... If I split them into their own series I can get them to match better but then I have a two series for the title." No maintainer reply. — [Komf #290](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/issues/290)
- Omnibus as N volumes (Komf, open since July 2023): request to "Treat Omnibus as 2 volumes", with a commenter noting 3-in-1 also exists. — [Komf #81](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/issues/81)
- Multi-volume omnibus filenames (Kavita, closed, April 2026): "Multi-volume filename parsing of Carthago Omnibus fails to be recognized as multiple volumes". Related 2024 issues: files with "Omnibus" in the name missing from OPDS, and ComicVine-library series with Omnibus in the name. — [Kavita #4599](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/issues/4599), [Kavita #2888](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/issues/2888), [Kavita #2889](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/issues/2889)
- Number in title read as chapter (Kavita, open since September 2022): files such as `ACCA - 13-Territory Inspection Department v01`, `Brave 10 v01`, `Cyborg 009 v01`, `Boys Over Flowers Season 2 v01` show "as volume AND chapter (chapter is always the number in the series title)". Proposed rule: an unmarked number before the first volume marker is part of the title. — [Kavita #1534](https://github.com/Kareadita/Kavita/issues/1534)
- Edition code taken as title (Komf, open, September 2025): "Auto-identify sets Title to edition code (GN/HC) instead of book title". — [Komf #240](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf/issues/240)
- Non-numeric issue numbers (Mylar3, closed, February 2024): Btooom! has two endings, numbered on ComicVine as `26.Light` and `26.Dark`, and the series could not be added. — [Mylar3 #1507](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/issues/1507)
- Volume-granularity libraries versus chapter-granularity trackers (Mihon, open since January 2024), including the case where one library entry spans several tracker entries. — [Mihon #15](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/issues/15), [Mihon #3276](https://github.com/mihonapp/mihon/issues/3276)
- Wrong-volume binding at a series restart (Mylar3): mismatches "usually" happen "when a new volume of a series starts", and previously caused issues to be "downloaded that were for the wrong series". — [Mylar3 wiki: The mismatched status](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3/wiki/The-mismatched-status)
- ComicVine for manga returns foreign-language and omnibus entries ahead of the wanted one: a user matching Fullmetal Alchemist got German metadata first, and elsewhere "the omnibus edition, the wrong volume, or nothing at all." (Forum user report, surfaced via search snippet; the thread itself was not opened.) — [ComicVine forums](https://comicvine.gamespot.com/app.php/profile/Shawn7656/forums/)

### Inferences
- Problems the field avoids instead of solving: mapping chapters to volumes per edition; holding omnibus and tankobon files in one series with correct per-file metadata; deciding automatically which provider edition a folder is. The standing advice in each case is to split into separate folders/series and encode the edition in the name.
- Failures cluster where a title-derived key is the identity. Kavita's 2022 edition collapse and the 2026 "Really-Shy" fix are the same bug class four years apart: normalisation strips the token that distinguished two works. Its July 2026 move toward external-ID-first lookup is the structural response.
- Open-for-years status on Komf #8 (2022), Komf #81 (2023), Kavita #1534 (2022) and Mihon #15 (2024) indicates these are hard, not merely unprioritised: each needs data (edition layouts, chapter-to-volume tables) that the tools do not have.

### Gaps
- No issue specifically about Re:ZERO arcs or JoJo parts being split or merged was found in the searches run; the split-series failure is evidenced only by analogous cases (Kamen Rider in Sonarr, Persona Q "Side P3/P4" parsing correctly in Kavita #1534).
- No issue specifically about coloured editions mis-matching was found; the only evidence is that Kavita defines the `Edition` relation for "special edition or colored edition".
- Issue searches were keyword-limited (omnibus, edition, volume, manga) across Kavita, Komf, Komga, Mylar3, Mihon and Stump; Komga returned nothing relevant for "omnibus", which may reflect its flat model more than an absence of the problem.
- Frequency of each failure is not quantified anywhere; the ranking in the takeaway reflects how thoroughly each is documented, not how often it occurs.
